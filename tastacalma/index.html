TastaCalma

A little pause. A cleaner keyboard.

  • A moment of care, without the fuss
  • Three simple steps
  • Small app, a little personality
  • Before you wipe
  • Help and privacy

TastaCalma gives your Mac’s keyboard a short, quiet break. Choose a timer, click Lock keyboard, and wipe the keys without sending stray letters into your work. Typing returns automatically when the countdown ends; your mouse or trackpad stays available throughout.

A small native Mac app, with a calm interface and a playful Italian touch.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store.

A moment of care, without the fuss

  • Your pace, your pause

    Choose 30 seconds, 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes. TastaCalma remembers your last choice, so your next cleaning break is ready when you are.

  • Quiet keys

    Temporarily pause keyboard input during the countdown. Your mouse and trackpad remain active, keeping the app’s controls within reach.

  • Back to typing

    The keyboard unlocks automatically when time runs out. Finished early? Click Unlock now and carry on with your day.

  • A comfortable appearance

    Choose System, Light or Dark theme, select your preferred language. Your preference is saved, and Reduce Motion brings a quieter presentation.

  • A helping hand

    Guided Accessibility setup explains the permission TastaCalma needs. Labelled controls and spoken lock and unlock announcements support VoiceOver use.

  • A private little ritual

    No account, ads or in-app analytics. TastaCalma works offline and does not record, store or transmit what you type.

Three simple steps

  1. Set up once. Enable TastaCalma in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. The app walks you through it, and you can choose Later to return when ready.
  2. Choose your pause. Select a duration, release any held modifier keys, and click Lock keyboard.
  3. Let the timer take care of the rest. Keep your mouse or trackpad nearby. Wait for automatic unlock, or click Unlock now to finish early.

Enabling Accessibility permission never starts a cleaning timer by itself. Each session begins with your click.

Small app, a little personality

Meet Mammamia, TastaCalma’s icon: an ivory keyboard, a sage-green cloth, and a tiny Italian flourish. A friendly reminder that even a well-used keyboard deserves a moment of attention.

TastaCalma follows your Mac’s language preferences, with English and translations into Italian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. You can choose your preferred language independently.

Before you wipe

TastaCalma pauses typing; it is a cleaning aid, not a security lock. Power / Touch ID cannot be blocked, and some hardware or system actions may still respond. Secure Keyboard Entry or a system interruption can end a session early: stop cleaning if the app reports that the keyboard has unlocked.

Follow your hardware manufacturer’s cleaning instructions. See Apple’s guidance on cleaning Apple products , including when to shut down and unplug your Mac.

Help and privacy

Visit TastaCalma Support for permission setup, troubleshooting and contact details. Read the Privacy Policy for how the app handles keyboard events and local preferences.
