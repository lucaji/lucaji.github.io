SyntoniaRituals is now on the App Store

Singing bowls, personal rituals, and sound in motion

Luca Cipressi  included in Software and Code
 22 September 2026  218 words   2 minutes 

SyntoniaRituals is now available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, released today, September 22, 2026. It is a tactile musical space built around singing bowls, slow listening, and the small gestures that bring a sound to life. Strike a bowl, trace its rim, or let a resonance fade into silence: each movement becomes part of the music.

The bowls are synthesised in real time, with material-inspired voices and shifting overtones that respond to how you play. Shape their character, add space with reverb and delay, and explore the balance between a clear strike and a gently sustained tone. On compatible iPads, Apple Pencil offers another way to give those gestures expression.

Alongside live playing, the Ritual sequencer lets you arrange strikes, continuous rubbing, and pauses into a sound experience that unfolds at your own pace. You can play along as the sequence progresses or follow the sound through responsive visuals, where resonance becomes moving colour and geometry. It is a place for quiet exploration, personal listening sessions, and musical ideas that need room to breathe.

SyntoniaRituals starts with a free, playable experience. An optional one-time Pro purchase expands the instruments, sound editing, Rituals, and visualizers, with no subscription. For screenshots, the full feature overview, and the App Store download link, visit the SyntoniaRituals product page .
