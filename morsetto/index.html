Morsetto

A few words. A rhythm of your own.

  • Choose your starting point

  • Shape the way it moves
  • Start free, make the phrase your own with Pro
  • Made for your music host

Morsetto turns a short word or phrase into a Morse-derived rhythm, then lets you shape its spacing, movement and expression. A name, an idea or a few letters can become the pulse running through your music.

Two complementary plug-ins share that approach. MorsettoAudio shapes the sound on an audio track, following a Morse pattern, a loop ramp or the dynamics of the main audio; Pro also enables a separate Sidechain input. MorsettoMidi brings rhythmic articulation and expression to the notes you play.

Coming soon for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Each product works inside a compatible music host.

Choose your starting point

  • MorsettoAudio

    Give a pad, recording or instrument output a rhythmic contour. Map five Free modulation sources, plus Sidechain with Pro, independently to amplitude, filter cutoff, stereo balance, width and drive.

    Explore MorsettoAudio

  • MorsettoMidi

    Play notes or chords and let the pattern shape their expression. Sustain a phrase with continuous movement, or retrigger held notes at each mark. Shape velocity and up to four continuous controller or polyphonic-pressure mappings to suit your instrument.

Find a rhythm in your words

Begin with the complete MORSE pattern in the free version. Choose a Morse-unit duration from 1/64 to a whole note, with optional triplets. Your host supplies tempo and transport.

Pro lets you enter up to 20 characters, excluding ordinary spaces, and apply the text to reveal its dots, dashes and gaps. Move the first character to add leading silence, adjust later characters to reshape the gaps, and select or move a loop around the part you want to hear. Optional snapping follows the displayed Morse or host grid.

Shape the way it moves

Both products offer six Shape controls: dot/dash balance, attack and release, their edge offsets, and modulation depth. Keep a crisp pulse or let the transitions soften, with optional overlap reshaping for fades that collide. Automate the six controls from your host while the phrase follows the song position.

Five permanent mapping tabs keep their settings when disabled. Select a tab to edit it or inspect its pattern overlay; every active mapping keeps working independently of the selected tab.

Each plug-in instance keeps its own settings. Save them with your host’s project or preset workflow; Pro lets you use different custom phrases across your arrangement.

Start free, make the phrase your own with Pro

Both products include unlimited use of MORSE with its original spacing and complete loop. Free includes all mapping slots and destinations, shaping controls, note values, triplets, host automation, grid/zoom navigation and host saving. Audio includes five Free modulation sources and the main-input follower; Sidechain requires Audio Pro. All current MIDI sources and mappings are Free.

An optional one-time Pro purchase unlocks custom text, character spacing, leading silence and loop editing in either product, plus Sidechain in MorsettoAudio. MorsettoAudio Pro and MorsettoMidi Pro are separate purchases. Purchase or restore Pro in the corresponding app, then return to your host.

Made for your music host

Morsetto is designed around AUv3 plug-ins on iPhone, iPad and native macOS. Your host provides source material, instruments, recording and arrangement; the accompanying apps provide an offline User Manual, version information and Pro purchase/restoration. They can remain closed while you use the plug-ins.

The current system requirement is iOS/iPadOS 18 or later and macOS 15 or later. The Audio and MIDI products require different host capabilities; check the relevant support page before choosing a workflow. A host/device compatibility list will be added before release.

Help and privacy

Visit MorsettoAudio Support or MorsettoMidi Support for setup and troubleshooting. The shared Privacy Policy covers both products and explains the differences between their audio and MIDI processing.
