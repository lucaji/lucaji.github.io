Singing bowls for stillness

SyntoniaRituals is a tactile musical space for iPhone and iPad: play responsive singing bowls, shape their character, arrange evolving sequences, and let sound become movement and light.

It is made for slow listening and small gestures. Strike a bowl. Trace its rim. Leave a moment of silence. Build a ritual and allow it to unfold.

An instrument shaped around you

Begin with a focused Free experience. Expand your collection and creative range with a one-time Pro purchase, whenever you are ready.

  • Bowls with a living voice

    Discover the warmth of bronze, the clarity of quartz crystal, and the character of brass, copper, steel, aluminium, glass and ceramic. Material-inspired synthesis brings shifting overtones, gentle beating and naturally fading resonance to every bowl.

    Free
    Bronze Reverie and Ceramic Earth.
    Pro
    All eight material presets.

  • Expression at your fingertips

    Strike, circle and pinch to shape sound as you play. The interactive rim-pad follows your circular speed; optional position controls vary strike strength and rubbing pressure. Choose Resonant, Aurora, Kinetic or Minimal visuals to accompany each gesture.

    Free & Pro
    Touch expression and all four rim visual styles.

  • Your own space to play

    Build a personal arrangement of resonant voices, each with its own pitch, colour and character. Play by hand while a Ritual unfolds, adding a spontaneous response to the sequence.

    Free
    One playable Live Rig bowl.
    Pro
    Layer up to four Live Rig bowls.

  • Comfort for either hand

    Place your instruments where they feel most natural. Swap the Live Rig and sequencer sides in landscape, or bring the bowls closer by placing the Live Rig at the bottom in portrait.

    Free & Pro
    Both layout options, for left- or right-handed playing.

  • Apple Pencil expression

    On a compatible iPad, a pressure-sensitive Pencil adds nuance to strike brightness and rubbing. Pro lets you assign supported pressure, tilt, direction, rotation, hover and squeeze inputs to expressive sound controls. Available gestures depend on your iPad and Pencil model.

    Free
    Pencil playing with the default pressure response.
    Pro
    Advanced Mapping with adjustable sensitivity.

  • Compose your own Rituals

    Arrange single or repeating strikes, continuous rubbing and silence. Choose each step’s duration, bowl and frequency, then let tones fade naturally, dampen or overlap as the sequence progresses.

    Free
    An editable Default Ritual with up to five steps.
    Pro
    Named Rituals with up to 64 steps each.

  • Shape every resonance

    Move from a gentle change of character to detailed sound design. Explore strike attack, brightness, overtones, beating, drift and rim friction, or introduce the shifting colour of a fluid-filled bowl.

    Free
    Character, Bloom and Motion controls, plus six voicings.
    Pro
    Full sound editing, fluid simulations and saved personal bowls.

  • Space, echoes, atmosphere

    Give each gesture room to linger. The built-in stereo FX stack combines natural-sounding reverb and delay, with adjustable room size, decay, damping, echo time, feedback and tone.

    Free & Pro
    The complete reverb and delay controls, with remembered settings.

  • Frequencies with context

    Explore five curated categories: Astronomical Cycles, Solfeggio, Astrology, Tuning Forks and 12-TET Tuning (A440). Informative text accompanies every entry, offering musical, historical or cultural context for your listening choices.

    Free
    Browse every description and play six curated references.
    Pro
    Full selection, frequency readouts and custom presets.

  • See the sound unfold

    Follow your music through luminous ribbons, unfolding geometry and flowing colour. Personalise palettes, motion, glow and audio response; Reduce Motion brings a calmer visual presentation.

    Free
    Harmonic Spectrum and Resonance Mandala.
    Pro
    All ten fullscreen visualizers.

SyntoniaRituals grows from the instrument-design and acoustic-synthesis work behind SyntoniaLive, with its own focus on expressive singing bowls, personal rituals and sound in motion. SyntoniaLive is coming to the App Store, come back checking soon!

A playable Live Rig

The Free version begins with one singing bowl in the Live Rig; the permanent Pro unlock expands the surface to as many as four. Choose a pitch, colour and acoustic character for each bowl, then bring it to life with a touch.

  • Strike a bowl for an immediate resonant response.
  • Rub around its rim to let the sound gather and bloom.
  • Move between bowls while a Ritual continues to play.
  • Pinch to gently dampen a ringing bowl.

Every voice is synthesised in real time, responding to the movement of your hand with a changing blend of attack, overtones and resonance. A strike gives way to shifting overtones; a circular gesture lets the tone gather and sustain.

Shape a bowl of your own

Begin with one of eight material-inspired Singing Bowls and explore six voicings. Character, Bloom and Motion offer an immediate way to shape the sound, from a softer presence to a brighter, more animated resonance.

Free includes Bronze Reverie and Ceramic Earth, with sound adjustments you can keep in your Live Rig. Pro opens the complete material collection, detailed sound controls and fluid-filled bowl simulations, and lets you save a personal collection in the Library.

Explore the Frequency Catalogue for a new listening direction. Browse the stories and reference notes in either version, play the six curated Free choices, or unlock the complete catalogue and create custom references with Pro.

Compose time, gesture, and silence

The Ritual sequencer turns a listening idea into an ordered performance. Each step can hold a duration and one of three actions:

  • Timed Strikes repeat a bowl at a chosen interval, or sound it once.
  • Continuous Rubbing sustains the gesture around the rim.
  • Silence gives resonance and attention room to settle.

Choose a bowl and frequency for each sounding step, adjust the timing and transitions, and drag steps into the order you want. Play, pause or move between steps, and add your own gestures in the Live Rig while the Ritual progresses. The Free version provides an editable Default Ritual with up to five steps. Pro extends a program to 64 steps and adds named Rituals with their own colour and notes.

See the sound take form

The fullscreen visualizer turns your playing into a changing field of light. Choose an atmosphere to match the moment:

Harmonic Spectrum and Resonance Mandala are included in the Free version. The permanent Pro unlock makes all ten visualizers selectable.

Make the view your own with colour palettes, backgrounds, motion, glow and audio response, alongside controls suited to each visualizer. Your choices are remembered for the next session. With Reduce Motion enabled on your device, movement becomes gentler and visual activity more restrained.

Designed for listening and performance

Make room for quiet listening, sound exploration, creative work or an intimate performance. SyntoniaRituals invites you to follow the resonance, choose your own pace and leave space between gestures.

  • No account is required.
  • No microphone recording is used.
  • Your sounds, Rituals and preferences are stored locally, with no in-app cloud sync.
  • VoiceOver labels, adaptable text and Reduce Motion settings support a more comfortable experience.

Listen at a comfortable volume, especially during long sessions.

Availability

SyntoniaRituals is being prepared for its first App Store release on iPhone and iPad. The Free version is a complete playable experience; an optional one-time Pro purchase expands the instrument, Library, Ritual sequencer, and visualizer selection. There is no subscription.
