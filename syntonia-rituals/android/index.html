SyntoniaRituals for Android

Singing bowls for stillness · Under development

SyntoniaRituals for Android is under development. The features below describe the current development version and may change before release.

A space for bowls and Rituals

  • Play a singing bowl. Strike, rub, and dampen a bowl in its focused performance view.
  • Shape its character. Explore Character, Bloom, Motion, and Breath controls, with reverb to give the sound space.
  • Compose a Ritual. Arrange single strikes, repeating strikes, continuous rubbing, and silence into a timed sequence.
  • Explore your Library. Browse bowls and the Frequency Catalogue, and organise your saved Rituals.
  • Follow the resonance. A ring visualizer responds to the sound.

The Android edition has its own presentation and sound controls. Its current audio playback runs while the app is in the foreground.

Free and Pro in development

The current Free experience provides one Root bowl in the Live Rig and an editable Default Ritual with up to five eligible steps. Pro adds saved bowl editing, custom frequencies, and named Rituals with up to 64 steps.

Final release details, screenshots, and download information will be added here when available.

Support and privacy

For questions about the Android edition, visit Android support or email lucaji.milletgrain@gmail.com .

The shared SyntoniaRituals Privacy Policy covers the Android edition and identifies platform-specific data handling.
